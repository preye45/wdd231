Flow-hub- Site Plan

Site Name

Site Name: Flower-Hub

This name reflects the beauty and community of florists coming together to showcase their businesses to the world.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this site is to help people and businesses grow and understand the beauty in flowers and the environment at large.

Scenarios

  • What species of flowers would be available?

Color Schema

  • Primary Color: #4A7C59 (Earthy Green) – Used for headings, buttons, and accents.
  • Accent Color: #E8DAB2 (Light Sand) – Used for backgrounds and section highlights.

Typography

  • Merriweather – Used for all headings to convey a sense of tradition and reflection.
  • Open Sans – Used for body text for readability and a clean modern look.
